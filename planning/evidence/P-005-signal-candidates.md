# P-005 Evidence — candidate variables for very new Pump.fun tokens

- Accessed: 2026-08-25

**Scope:** variables observable in the creation transaction or within seconds/minutes.  Sources are limited to official protocol/program repositories, Solana RPC specifications, official provider documentation, and Metaplex specifications/source. PumpPortal is a direct third-party provider source, **not** Pump protocol authority.

**Pump source snapshot:** [`pump-fun/pump-public-docs` commit `9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333`](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/tree/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333).

## Evidence boundary

The primary sources establish field semantics and operational availability. **None supplies an empirical, out-of-sample study showing that any variable below predicts a “promising” or “poor” Pump.fun launch.** Therefore:

- **Validated availability** means a field/state/event is explicitly documented by the protocol, RPC specification, or provider. It does not mean predictive value is validated.
- **Derived, plausible only** means the value is a deterministic, point-in-time calculation from validated fields, but neither its definition nor predictive value is endorsed by the source.
- **Heuristic only** means it attempts actor attribution (bot, sybil, insider, wash flow). Address-level evidence does not establish beneficial ownership or intent.

No threshold is recommended. Any future threshold requires a separately defined outcome label, point-in-time historical dataset, leakage controls, and out-of-sample validation.

### G. Bot, sybil, and coordinated-flow proxies

No allowed primary source labels beneficial owners, bots, sybils, or wash traders. Every variable here is explicitly heuristic.

| Variable | Precise definition | Data source | Availability/cost | Failure/manipulation modes | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| same-slot buyer share | Fraction of early buy quote/tokens in creation slot or first declared N slots; number of distinct trade users there | Pump `TradeEvent`; Solana `getTransaction`/`getBlock` | Once slots settle; transaction/block index | Legitimate demand and bots both burst; many wallets per actor; same slot is not coordination proof | **Heuristic only** |
| early-wallet prior-activity distribution | Per early user, earliest retrievable signature and pre-t0 signature count; aggregate only after declaring archival coverage | Solana `getSignaturesForAddress`/`getTransaction` | Potentially many archival calls; high cost/rate-limit exposure | No wallet birth timestamp; archival truncation; seasoned wallets; program/router addresses | **Heuristic only** |
| common-funder graph | Trace documented pre-t0 SOL transfer edges to fixed depth/time and compute shared immediate-funder share; preserve edges rather than collapsing identity | Solana transaction account keys, balances, inner instructions | After early users known; expensive archival decoding | Exchanges, bridges, faucets, relayers, and tooling cause false positives; multi-hop funding causes false negatives | **Heuristic only** |
| ATA-created-and-bought-in-same-transaction share | Fraction of early buyer transactions that create the mint’s token account and buy in the same transaction | Solana transaction instructions/inner instructions; associated-token account semantics ([Solana token basics](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics)) | Per transaction | Ordinary first purchase also creates ATA; routers/paymasters; parser misses inner instructions | **Heuristic only** |

| rapid round-trip proxy | Per declared user, buy→sell cycles and mechanically matched token quantity within W | Pump `TradeEvent` | End W | Legitimate trading/market making; multi-wallet evasion; matching algorithm sensitivity | **Heuristic only; not proof of wash trading** |

## Provider-specific implementation notes

### Helius

- [`POST /v0/transactions`](https://www.helius.dev/docs/api-reference/enhanced-transactions/gettransactions) parses known signatures, but retains confirmed/finalized rather than processed semantics.
- [Enhanced transactions by address](https://www.helius.dev/docs/api-reference/enhanced-transactions/gettransactionsbyaddress) can enrich address history; it does not solve beneficial-owner attribution.
- [Webhooks](https://www.helius.dev/docs/webhooks) can monitor addresses, but address watchlists do not by themselves discover every future Pump mint. Handle duplicate delivery and RPC backfill after retry exhaustion.
- DAS [`getAsset`](https://www.helius.dev/docs/api-reference/das/getasset) is useful for metadata, authority, mutability, supply, token-program and mint/freeze-authority fields. Gate interpretation on `last_indexed_slot`; do not use its 600-second cached price as a creation-second signal.
- DAS [`getTokenAccounts`](https://www.helius.dev/docs/api-reference/das/gettokenaccounts) supports paginated SPL-token account retrieval; completeness is still index-bound.

### Birdeye

- Creation: [`GET /defi/token_creation_info`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-defi-token_creation_info), documented 50 CU, Lite–Enterprise.
- Security/authority/concentration: [`GET /defi/token_security`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-defi-token_security), documented 25 CU, Lite–Enterprise.
- Current market and short-window activity: [`GET /defi/token_overview`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-defi-token_overview), documented 20 CU, Starter–Enterprise.
- Holder distribution: [`GET /holder/v1/distribution`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-holder-v1-distribution), documented 35 CU; V3 holder rows: [`GET /defi/v3/token/holder`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-defi-v3-token-holder).
- Discovery: [`GET /defi/v2/tokens/new_listing`](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-defi-v2-tokens-new_listing) and WebSocket [`SUBSCRIBE_TOKEN_NEW_LISTING`](https://docs.birdeye.so/docs/websocket) represent provider-recognized listings, not necessarily the Pump creation transaction.

### Shyft

- Official Pump examples: [new-token detection](https://docs.shyft.to/solana-yellowstone-grpc/examples/pumpfun/solana-grpc-pumpfun-new-token-detection), [transaction parsing](https://docs.shyft.to/solana-yellowstone-grpc/examples/pumpfun/solana-grpc-pumpfun-transaction-parsing), [buy/sell detection](https://docs.shyft.to/solana-yellowstone-grpc/examples/pumpfun/solana-grpc-pumpfun-detect-transaction-type), and [migration detection](https://docs.shyft.to/solana-yellowstone-grpc/examples/pumpfun/solana-grpc-pumpfun-detect-token-migration).
- Treat sample extraction such as `postTokenBalances[0]` as an example, not a protocol invariant; production should decode Pump instructions/events and retain parser/IDL version.
- [`GET /sol/v1/transaction/parsed`](https://docs.shyft.to/solana-apis/transactions/transaction-apis) exposes payer/signers/actions/raw balances/logs/slot, but documented short history makes immediate archival important.

### QuickNode

- Standard Solana HTTP/WSS semantics remain those of canonical RPC.
- [Solana gRPC](https://www.quicknode.com/docs/solana/solana-grpc/overview) streams transactions/accounts/slots and is documented as included with Scale/Business plans.
- [`POST /pump-fun/quote`](https://www.quicknode.com/docs/solana/pump-fun-quote) can measure a declared-size contemporaneous quote; store input/output mint, amount, time, context, fees and response. It is not an execution or stable liquidity measurement.

### Metaplex

- Distinguish on-chain account data (`name`, `symbol`, `uri`, creators, update authority, mutability) from off-chain JSON/media fetched through the URI.
- “Verified creator” means the address signed/verified its association, not external identity verification.
- A mutable metadata account can change; an immutable account can still point to unavailable or externally mutable content.

## Research conclusion

The strongest measurement provenance is the finalized Pump/PumpSwap event and account state, with Solana RPC used to reconstruct transactions, balances, token accounts, and histories. Low-latency processed feeds are useful triggers, not canonical truth. Creator history, initial buy, curve progress, early flow, holders, metadata, migration, and liquidity are all measurable candidates; bot/sybil features remain attribution heuristics. **No allowed primary source validates predictive value or justifies a numeric cutoff for any candidate.**
