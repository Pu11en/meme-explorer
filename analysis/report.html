Fraud Detection Rules (v2)

DISTRIBUTED BUNDLE — same_slot_buys ≥ 5
Multiple wallets coordinated to buy in the same block as coin creation. Inflates unique buyer count artificially.
SINGLE-WALLET BUNDLE — top_wallet_pct ≥ 40%
One wallet holds 40%+ of first-minute volume.
CONTROLLED — unique_buyers ≤ 6 AND sniper_ratio ≥ 80%
A small bot cabal controls the majority of buys. No real organic activity.

Key Patterns

Migration rate is ~2.4% every hour — about 34 coins per 1,400 launches, completely consistent across batches.
The real-time trigger: ≥50 unique buyers in first 60s + same_slot_buys < 5 → 33% chance of migration. Fires ~12 times per hour.
Snipers chase winners. 33/34 migrators each hour had known sniper bots pile in. High sniper presence is a signal, not a red flag.
Repeat devs relaunch every session. Track dev wallets — when a known dev gets organic traction, they relaunch immediately after. "Luke" did it twice in one hour, both migrated under 2 min.
Sub-2-minute migrations are unmistakable — 400–900 buys, 200–600 unique buyers in the first minute. You see it in the feed before any algorithm would catch it.
