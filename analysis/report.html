DISTRIBUTED BUNDLE — same_slot_buys ≥ 5
Multiple wallets coordinated to buy in the same block as coin creation. Inflates unique buyer count artificially.

One wallet holds 40%+ of first-minute volume.

A small bot cabal controls the majority of buys. No real organic activity.
