# Pump recorder candidate — adversarial verification

**Cutoff:** 2026-08-26 12:00 UTC. **Evidence boundary:** owner docs/schema and bounded no-key reads. Live results below are point observations, not SLAs. The protocol contract is pinned to [`pump-public-docs@9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333`](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/tree/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333); this IDL/docs snapshot is not deployed-bytecode proof.

## Verdict and corrections

The candidate’s **local chain-verification design is sound**, but its provider disposition must be stricter:

1. **Only Solana chain data adds correctness-critical facts beyond PumpAPI.** Derive the Pump curve PDA, Pump `pool-authority`, and PumpSwap index-0 WSOL pool locally; validate finalized account owner, discriminator, bump, mints, creator, LP mint, and transaction `meta.err`. PumpAPI remains a hint, never authority.
2. **Completion is not migration.** Completion is finalized curve state `complete == true && real_token_reserves == 0`; migration is separately evidenced by the locally derived canonical Pool account and, where retained, a successful finalized `migrate` transaction plus `CompletePumpAmmMigrationEvent`. `getTransaction: null` means unavailable/not found at that commitment, not “not migrated.” [Pinned Pump README](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333/docs/PUMP_PROGRAM_README.md#L145-L182) · [Pump IDL](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333/idl/pump.json) · [Solana `getTransaction`](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction)
3. **Canonical means exact PDA, not same pair.** For the pinned SOL migration path: `pool_authority=PDA_Pump(["pool-authority",mint])`; `pool=PDA_PumpSwap(["pool",u16_le(0),pool_authority,mint,WSOL])`. PumpSwap permits other creator/index/pair combinations. Do not generalize this pin to non-WSOL migration. [Pump migrate constraints](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333/idl/pump.json#L2994-L3137) · [PumpSwap pool rules](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/9c82f61cb711b044a17f770ab8ce9f9bdf78f333/docs/PUMP_SWAP_README.md#L63-L137)
4. **Reject PumpPortal as a recorder dependency.** Its free new-token/migration methods duplicate PumpAPI; trade streams require a key, a linked wallet funded with at least 0.02 SOL, and metering. It is live-only at `processed`, with no published output schema, replay cursor, completeness, or finality SLA. Its terms are revocable and restrict copying/distribution. [Real-time API](https://pumpportal.fun/data-api/real-time/) · [fees](https://pumpportal.fun/fees/) · [FAQ](https://pumpportal.fun/FAQ/) · [terms](https://pumpportal.fun/legal/)
5. **Reject Jupiter from strict-free core.** Its distinct value is amount/time-specific cross-router output, not migration or pool identity. Keyless requests are framed for testing/prototyping; `/swap/v2/order` is credit-classified, and owner terms restrict API-content/performance use. `transaction:null` without a taker is quote-only; missing route/metadata is not “not migrated.” [Setup](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/portal/setup) · [plans](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/portal/plans) · [order](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/swap/order-and-execute) · [license](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/legal/sdk-api-license-agreement)
6. **DEX Screener is optional, nullable enrichment only.** Distinct fields are indexed USD/native price, rolling activity/volume, reported liquidity, FDV/market cap, and pair age. They are not raw chain truth, executable depth, replay, or canonical identity. Listing requires a pool plus a transaction, but no indexing/persistence/completeness bound exists; owner terms limit competition/redistribution and allow termination. [API reference](https://docs.dexscreener.com/api/reference) · [listing](https://docs.dexscreener.com/token-listing) · [API terms](https://docs.dexscreener.com/api/api-terms-and-conditions)
7. **No strict-free reliability claim survives.** PumpAPI is as-is/as-available and may suspend/discontinue; Solana public RPC is rate-limited, subject to change, non-archival, and explicitly not intended for production. The supportable claim is **bounded finalized verification with explicit unknown/gap states**, not continuous or complete recording. [PumpAPI legal](https://pumpapi.io/legal) · [PumpAPI stream](https://pumpapi.io/stream) · [Solana public clusters](https://solana.com/docs/references/clusters)

## Exact missing/failure semantics to retain

- Solana `getAccountInfo`/`getMultipleAccounts` account `null`: absent at that endpoint/commitment; `getTransaction` result `null`: transaction not found at requested commitment. Preserve RPC error, HTTP 403/429/5xx, timeout, decode failure, unknown discriminator, and pruned history separately. [Account RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/getaccountinfo) · [multiple accounts](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/getmultipleaccounts) · [transaction RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction)
- PumpAPI replay HTTP 404: archive object unavailable, not “no chain events.” WSS disconnect/gap/order/reorg semantics are undocumented; reconnect and reconcile. [Replay](https://pumpapi.io/historical-replay) · [stream](https://pumpapi.io/stream)
- DEX Screener `200` with `pairs:null`, `pairs:[]`, or nullable fields: no indexed result/value in that response, not chain nonexistence. Keep 400/404/429/5xx and transport failures distinct.
- Jupiter quote success, build success/failure, no route, invalid input, throttling, and omitted token metadata are separate states; no one missing field proves no migration.

The historical transaction was returned three times at slot `388775295` with `meta.err=null`.

These probes also exposed why third-party states must remain non-authoritative: three DEX Screener exact-pair requests returned `200 {"pairs":null,"pair":null}` even though an earlier repository probe had observed that pair indexed; three requests for PumpAPI replay hour `2026/05/09/19` returned HTTP `404`; three no-key Jupiter orders returned HTTP 200 quote-only responses with `transaction:null`. This is repeatability evidence for the verifier and concrete missing-state behavior—not availability, latency, completeness, or correctness statistics.

## Recorder acceptance rule

Persist raw provider frame/account/transaction, endpoint, receipt time, slot/commitment, signature plus event ordinal, `meta.err`, PDA seeds/bumps, decoder and IDL commit/hash, and an explicit state: `incomplete`, `completed_not_migrated`, `canonical_pool_verified`, `migration_tx_verified`, `provider_missing`, `rpc_history_unavailable`, `decode/schema_error`, or `gap_unknown`. Reject trials/credits/CUs, funded-wallet paths, paid token-info/trade calls, marketing claims, undocumented scraping, duplicate launch-only feeds, and every unsupported reliability assertion.
